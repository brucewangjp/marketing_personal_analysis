# First-Party Survey Principles

- Version: v0.1
- Status: Draft
- Updated: 2026-08-23

## Goal

Collect direct, voluntary responses that help explain group-level attitudes, motivations, concerns, and stated intentions toward a company, industry, product, or topic.

## What the survey should measure

- Awareness and familiarity.
- Positive, neutral, or negative attitude.
- Confidence, uncertainty, interest, and concern.
- Reasons behind the respondent’s view.
- Stated intention, such as learning more, buying, holding, avoiding, or waiting, when relevant to the research context.

## What the survey must not do

- Diagnose mental-health conditions or infer private psychological traits.
- Collect unnecessary sensitive personal data.
- Present a small or biased sample as representative of all consumers or investors.
- State that intentions guarantee future actions.

## Required report metadata

Every survey-based result must show:

- Number of respondents.
- Recruitment method.
- Field dates.
- Target population and any segmentation used.
- Question wording or a link to it.
- Material limitations and uncertainty.

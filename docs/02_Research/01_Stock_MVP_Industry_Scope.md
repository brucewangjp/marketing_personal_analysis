# Stock MVP Industry Scope: AI and Semiconductors

- Version: v0.1
- Status: Approved for MVP
- Updated: 2026-08-23

## 1. Decision

The first Stock Analysis MVP focuses on US-listed companies in two connected areas:

1. Artificial intelligence.
2. Semiconductors and semiconductor equipment.

This is a research scope, not an investment recommendation or a list of securities to buy or sell.

## 2. Included company themes

### AI

- AI infrastructure, including computing and data-center capacity.
- AI software and platforms.
- Enterprise adoption of AI where it is material to the company’s reported business.

### Semiconductors

- Chip designers and manufacturers.
- Semiconductor equipment and materials.
- Semiconductor-related components and supply-chain companies.

## 3. Research questions the MVP should support

- How does the company describe its AI or semiconductor exposure in SEC filings?
- What revenue, cost, capital-expenditure, risk, or demand signals are disclosed?
- What has changed across recent reporting periods?
- Which macroeconomic and semiconductor-industry indicators are relevant context?
- Which questions remain unanswered by the available evidence?

## 4. Initial FRED context

The report should be able to incorporate, where relevant:

- Interest-rate and inflation indicators.
- Employment and general economic activity indicators.
- Semiconductor and electronic-component industrial-production indicators.
- Semiconductor manufacturing capacity-utilization indicators.

The system must identify the selected series, frequency, and observation period in the final report.

## 5. Exclusions for the first release

- Non-US reporting regimes unless separately added.
- Cryptocurrency, foreign exchange, and commodities analysis.
- Real-time pricing and trading signals.
- Predictions of security prices.
- A claim that every company mentioning AI is an AI-focused company.

## 6. Next decision

Select a small initial set of US-listed companies for the first demonstration reports. The selection should represent different parts of the AI and semiconductor value chain.
